# Privacy Policy for Arrowhift

Last updated: November 2026
Developer: VxStudios (https://github.com)

VxStudios ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Arrowhift mobile application (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our Game.

## 1. Information We Collect Automatically
We do not collect any personal data (such as your name, email address, or phone number) directly. However, to ensure game stability and support development, our third-party services automatically collect minimal technical metrics:

* **Firebase Crashlytics:** Collects anonymized crash logs, device models, operating system versions, and processing performance data to help us track down bugs and fix game stability.
* **Google AdMob:** Collects standard device parameters and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID) to support ad delivery, frequency capping, and operational analytics.

## 2. How We Use Information
The minimal data processed is used solely to:
* Monitor game stability, catch system exceptions, and improve gameplay performance.
* Manage ad delivery mechanisms so players are not repeatedly shown identical ad creative loops.

## 3. Third-Party Links & Services
Our Game integrates APIs from third-party suppliers who maintain their own distinct privacy governance models. We encourage you to review their respective disclosures:
* Google Play Services: https://google.com
* Google AdMob: https://google.com
* Firebase Crashlytics: https://google.com

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our Game is targeted toward individuals aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly harvest or process identifiers belonging to children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently processed baseline device analytics from a minor, please contact us immediately so we can isolate and clear the log files.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, you can open an issue on our official public GitHub project profile at https://github.com/VxStudioss , https://github.com/VxStudioss/Arrowhift.
